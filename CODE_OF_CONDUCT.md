# Code of Conduct

Hivemind is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and constructive community.
This code applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other
project spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith while discussing technical differences.
- Focus criticism on ideas, behavior, and evidence rather than people.
- Share relevant context, acknowledge mistakes, and help make conversations
  accessible to participants with different backgrounds.
- Respect privacy and do not publish credentials, personal data, or confidential
  vulnerability details.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, deliberate
disruption, sexualized conduct without consent, and publishing private or
security-sensitive information are not acceptable. Attempts to evade a
moderation decision or retaliate against a good-faith report are also not
acceptable.

## Reporting concerns

Do not include sensitive details in a public issue. Report harassment, doxxing,
threats, or other abusive behavior through
[GitHub's abuse-reporting form](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse),
including links to the relevant repository content. The project does not
advertise a separate confidential conduct inbox.

Suspected software vulnerabilities or security/privacy defects must instead
follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and use the
[private GitHub Security Advisory form](https://github.com/Lesur-ai/hivemind/security/advisories/new).
See [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md) for non-sensitive product questions.

GitHub handles reports submitted through its abuse form. Maintainers will
review concerns available to the project fairly, protect confidentiality where
possible, and may remove content, restrict participation, or take other
proportionate action. The project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
